Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At AxivoraX, accessible from axivorax.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AxivoraX and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account or join our waitlist, we may collect personal information such as:

  • Email address
  • Account authentication credentials (securely stored via Supabase)
  • Trading performance data and metrics you input into your journal

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain your AxivoraX dashboard
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our platform features
  • Send you important platform updates, security alerts, and support messages

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures through secure cloud databases (Supabase) to protect your personal and trading information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@axivorax.com.
